Discovery

Great Android apps are scattered everywhere. AppHarbor brings them together.

Plenty of excellent apps never land in a mainstream store. They ship from a developer's GitHub releases, a small F-Droid repository, Codeberg, GitLab, or a self-hosted forge. AppHarbor turns those scattered release pages into a library you can actually keep.

  • Follow a creator once. Discover Android projects across an entire developer account.
  • Track one repository. Keep a single project when you do not want the whole account.
  • Browse repositories normally. F-Droid-style catalogs live beside external forge releases.
  • Return to your library. The source is remembered, so future releases do not require another hunt.

More than another F-Droid client.

AppHarbor is evolving into a personal discovery and update layer for Android apps, regardless of which repository or software forge publishes them.

Multiple sources, one catalog

Browse F-Droid-style repositories and direct forge releases without treating them as separate worlds.

Creator discovery

Follow GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg, Gitea and Forgejo accounts and discover the Android apps they publish.

Personal library

Keep the obscure, useful and interesting Android projects you find instead of losing them in browser bookmarks.

Durable discovery

Creator scans checkpoint their progress, retry transient failures and resume after the app process is interrupted.

APK-aware

AppHarbor works with Android releases themselves, making independent projects feel like part of a coherent app catalog.

Local-first

Your library remains useful on-device. Optional multi-device sync is being designed as an addition, not a dependency.
